Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

YT Media Player Accelerator ("we", "our", or "the extension") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our extension and licensing system handle data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Hardware Identifier: A randomly generated anonymous string stored locally on your device to ensure license compliance (1 device per license).
  • License Key & Email: Your license key and email address provided during Shopify checkout for license verification.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your browsing history, visited websites, or YouTube search queries.
  • We do NOT sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do NOT use tracking cookies or advertising pixels in our extension.

4. Security

All license verification requests are encrypted via HTTPS and processed on Google Cloud Platform with native Google Cloud Firestore database encryption.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@forfor.site.
